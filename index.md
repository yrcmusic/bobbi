title: Bobbi AI Privacy Policy
---


_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

Bobbi AI ("Bobbi", "the bot", "we") is a Discord bot run by the staff of the **Blox Studio** Discord server. It answers questions about Blox Studio's Roblox games and chats with members.

## 1. Agreeing to this policy
By using Bobbi in any way, you agree to this policy. "Using Bobbi" includes:
- posting in any channel Bobbi is set up to read or reply in,
- @mentioning Bobbi, or replying to one of Bobbi's messages,
- sending Bobbi images or GIFs,
- using any of Bobbi's slash commands,
- boosting the server while Bobbi is in it.

If you don't agree, don't use Bobbi in any of these ways. You can keep using the rest of the server as normal.

You must follow Discord's Terms of Service to use Bobbi, including Discord's minimum age.

## 2. What Bobbi reads
Bobbi only reads:
- messages in channels staff have set up for Bobbi, including any channel where Bobbi reads recent messages for context,
- messages anywhere in the server that @mention Bobbi or reply to Bobbi,
- images, GIFs and link previews in those messages, when staff have turned image reading on,
- the message you replied to, when you mention or reply to Bobbi,
- your Discord user ID, username, display name and whether you boost the server.

Bobbi ignores every other message. It does not read direct messages.

## 3. How your data is used
Your data is used only to run Bobbi:
- **To reply:** your message, a few recent messages for context, and any image are sent to a third-party AI provider, which writes Bobbi's reply. We currently use Anthropic (Claude). We may switch to a different AI provider at any time without notice. We only use providers and plans that do **not** train their AI models on the data we send.
- **To apply limits and perks:** daily image limits, extra images staff give you, booster perks, cooldowns and spam checks.
- **To keep the server safe:** Bobbi follows the server's moderation rules and may refuse to answer.

We do **not**:
- save your messages to a database or file, or write them to our logs,
- train any AI model on your messages,
- use your data for profiling, advertising or analytics,
- sell, rent or give your data to anyone other than the AI provider that writes the reply.

## 4. Third parties
- **AI providers** receive the content described above to write replies. They handle it under their own privacy policies and may keep it for a short time for safety and abuse monitoring. Anthropic's policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- **Discord** handles all messages under Discord's own Privacy Policy.
- **Hosting:** Bobbi runs on a cloud server, where the data described in section 5 is stored.

Data may be processed in countries other than yours, including the United States.

## 5. What we store and for how long
- **Image allowance:** your user ID and how many images you used today (reset every day), plus any extra images staff gave you (kept until used).
- **Staff notes:** facts moderators type in with a slash command. They are not taken from member messages.
- **Usage totals:** token counts and estimated cost per month, with no message content or user info.
- **Short-term memory:** recent message text is held in memory for a few minutes (for example to stop the same question being spammed) and is cleared when the bot restarts.
- **Technical logs:** errors and status messages, with no message content.

We delete stored data when it's no longer needed or when you ask us to.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **Don't want Bobbi to read a message?** Don't post it in Bobbi's channels, and don't mention or reply to Bobbi.
- **Delete your data:** ask us (see section 9) and we'll delete what we store about you, such as your image allowance.
- **Questions or complaints:** contact us at any time.

## 7. Security
We keep stored data small, limit access to Blox Studio staff, and keep the bot's keys private. No system is perfectly secure, so don't send Bobbi passwords, personal details or anything private.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy at any time, for example if Bobbi gets new features or we switch AI provider. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you keep using Bobbi after a change, you agree to the updated policy.

## 9. Contact
For any issue, question or data request, message **yrc.** (display name **dophin**) directly on Discord, in the Blox Studio server.
